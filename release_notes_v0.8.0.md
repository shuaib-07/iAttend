## What's New in iAttend v0.8.0

### 🎓 Exam Period Support & Lifecycle
- **No Premature Auto-Archiving:** Semesters no longer aggressively archive themselves when classes end. Your active semester remains your primary workspace until you switch to a new semester or manually archive.
- **Dynamic Semester Status:** Shows "Exam Period" when regular classes have ended but exams remain, transitioning to "Active" once exams finish.
- **Past Semester Banner:** Viewing a previous concluded semester now shows a calm, non-alarming banner with a quick "Switch to Active" shortcut.

### 📝 Home Screen Exam Tracker
- **Upcoming Exams Section:** Displays all scheduled exams within the next 7 days with days countdown tags (*"Tomorrow"*, *"In 3 days"*).
- **Direct Quick Actions:** Mark exams as `Attempted` or `Missed` directly from the home screen card with haptic feedback.
- **Overdue Exam Prompts:** Automatically prompts you to record results for past un-entered exams.

### ⚖️ Attendance Exemptions & Weightage Toggle
- **Customizable Weightage:** Toggle attendance calculation on or off for recurring timetable slots (e.g. Library, Seminar), extra classes, and individual calendar classes.
- **Exam Week Extra Classes:** Extra classes held during exam prep are now properly included in attendance calculations.
